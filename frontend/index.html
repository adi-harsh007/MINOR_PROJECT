Dermatology Knowledge Base

Clinical reference manual for the 7 lesion classes recognized by the EfficientNet-B3 model architecture.

Critical Risk MEL

Melanoma

Malignant neoplasm of melanocytes. Requires urgent surgical excision and staging.

ABCDE CRITERIA

Asymmetry, Irregular Borders, Color Variation (>2 hues), Diameter >6mm, Evolving.

Low Risk NV

Melanocytic Nevus

Benign proliferation of melanocytes (common moles or birthmarks).

BENIGN MARKERS

Symmetrical shape, homogenous pigment network, sharp demarcation.

High Risk BCC

Basal Cell Carcinoma

Most common non-melanoma skin cancer. Slow growing, locally invasive.

DERMOSCOPIC KEYS

Arborizing telangiectatic vessels, translucent pearly papule, central ulceration.

Pre-Malignant AKIEC

Actinic Keratoses

Precancerous dysplastic epidermal lesions induced by chronic solar UV exposure.

CLINICAL PRESENTATION

Dry, scaly, erythematous macule or papule on sun-exposed head/extremities.

Low Risk BKL

Benign Keratosis

Seborrheic keratosis, solar lentigo, or lichen planus-like keratosis.

DERMOSCOPIC KEYS

Stuck-on warty appearance, comedo-like openings, milia-like cysts.

Low Risk DF

Dermatofibroma

Benign fibrous nodule occurring primarily on extremities of adults.

CLINICAL KEYS

Central white scar-like patch surrounded by delicate pigment network; positive dimple sign.

Low Risk VASC

Vascular Lesion

Cherry angiomas, angiokeratomas, pyogenic granulomas, or vascular malformations.

DERMOSCOPIC KEYS

Red, purple, or dark blue lacunae separated by fibrous septa.
